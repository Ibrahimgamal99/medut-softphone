  • Provisioning first, typing second3CX, Linkus, Wave and Acrobits all lead with QR or a link. A puts QR on top, B gives it a tab, C makes it a text button.
  • One address fieldZoiper takes user@server in one box and fills in the host. C does this. A and B keep separate fields.
  • Show the transport probeZoiper lists what it found on TLS, TCP and UDP. A shows the result as chips, B as a checkbox, C in one line of plain words.
  • Keep sign-in Advanced smallLinphone keeps only auth ID and proxies at sign-in, and Groundwire moves codecs, NAT and DTMF to account settings afterwards. Worth doing whichever direction you pick.
  • Name the reason, not the field"My PBX needs an authentication username or outbound proxy" (Zoiper) is clearer than "Advanced". C already writes this way.
  • Share a QR code without the passwordAcrobits QR codes can leave the password out so the app asks for it. That would make Medut's "share account" QR safer to send around.
