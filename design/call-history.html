Medut softphone · Pocket · Calls tab

Three ways to show call history

The same calls drawn three ways: a missed number you still owe, Mona you already called back, a recorded call with Sara, a shaky line from Reception, a message from Omar and a failed call from yesterday. Each design keeps the Pocket look and the 3-bar quality pill, and tapping a call still shows every figure the app saves.

Phones
A

Call back

Missed calls you haven't returned sit on top as a short to-do list. A missed call leaves it when you call that number back. Everything below is the plain list you have today.

Good for
A front desk or sales line, where every missed call needs an answer.
Gives up
The to-do block takes the top of the screen until it's empty.
B

People

One row per person, not per call. Their calls today show as small chips under the name, and messages sit in the same row. Tap the row to open their calls inline.

Good for
A team that calls the same extensions all day.
Gives up
One call takes an extra tap to reach.
C

Timeline

Your day on a time rail. Talk time is drawn to scale, recordings play from the rail, and a week strip jumps between days. Tapping a call opens a step-by-step record of it.

Good for
Reviewing a day and sorting out bad lines with the PBX admin.
Gives up
Fewer calls per screen, and the most custom drawing to build.

State 2 · after tapping Sara's recorded call

Tap a call

Each design opens the call differently. All three keep the full list of figures (MOS both ways, jitter, loss, round trip, codec, SRTP, ICE, Call-ID) one tap away.
